Findings
01

Your strongest story is missing from your own site.

Bisnow and the Houston Business Journal reported that Washington Ho of HBO Max's House of Ho is a co-founder, that CEO Kazi Ashik is positioning the store around biohacking and longevity, and that the partners are targeting 500 stores. That's exactly the kind of fact AI uses to describe a business. Your homepage doesn't mention any of it, and the page title is just "PINK FLORA CO." with no city or category.

Source · Bisnow, Hoodline, pinkfloraco.com
02

Your story is locked inside videos.

The homepage carries your grand-opening announcement and the "we saw the injunction coming" story, but only as video files. AI tools can't watch video. To them, that content doesn't exist.

Source · pinkfloraco.com homepage
03

Two phone numbers in circulation.

Your site lists 713-684-2874. A FiveStars rewards listing shows 832-725-8142. WheresWeed shows no phone and no linked socials. When sources disagree, AI either picks one at random or leaves the number out.

Source · pinkfloraco.com, FiveStars, WheresWeed
04

AI doesn't know which question you answer.

You position yourself as "not a smoke shop." Directories file you as a vape shop, a marijuana dispensary, or a general wellness store. Each label puts you in a different answer, and the vape-shop label works directly against the brand you built.

Source · FiveStars, WheresWeed, MapQuest
05

Not in the Houston "best dispensary" roundups.

A current guide to the best dispensaries in Houston features The Plug, Buds2Go and The Grow Room. Pink Flora isn't mentioned. These roundups are exactly what AI tools cite when someone asks for a recommendation.

Source · Houston dispensary guide (losgatan.com)
Already working
  • Hours and address are published in readable text on your Location page.
  • Clear category structure: CBD, mushroom, dispensary, kratom, biohacking, edibles.
  • Earned press most single-location shops never get.
  • A strong customer review already surfaces on MapQuest.
