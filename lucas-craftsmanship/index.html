Findings
01

Lucas Craftsmanship did not appear in the three builder searches we ran.

Searches for a custom home builder in the Heights, a historic bungalow renovation contractor in the Heights and a remodeler in 77018 showed Unika Homes, Houzz project photos, SERVPRO and Thumbtack lists. Your name search found you through Houzz, a Voyage Houston interview and the Heights Association directory, but lucasci.com itself was not in the results.

Web searches, checked Nov 16, 2026
02

The site tells three different stories about how long you have been in business.

The search description says "over twenty years", the homepage About section says "over twenty-five years" and the footer says "servicing Texas since 1994", which is over thirty. The Who We Are page adds "Relocating to Texas in 1991, Billy founded LCI." AI tools that see conflicting facts tend to repeat the smallest number or skip the claim.

lucasci.com and /who-we-are/, checked Nov 16, 2026
03

The search description shown on every page has typos and a stray word.

It reads "our our expertise in the industry allows us to handle" and "We have been featured on DYI on both House Crashers", and it ends with the word "Proof". The same text is used on the homepage, Who We Are and Contact pages, so it is often the first thing searchers read about you.

lucasci.com meta description, checked Nov 16, 2026
04

A visitor can't find an email address.

The Contact page has an embedded form and phone number, but no email address or email link. info@lucasci.com only appears in the site's behind the scenes business data. The Contact page also shows a second number, (713) 864-2560, with no label, and no page lists office hours.

lucasci.com/contact-us/, checked Nov 16, 2026
05

Your business data tells search tools your price range is "$500-$1000".

The structured data on the homepage lists priceRange as "$500-$1000". For a builder doing whole home renovations and new homes, that figure can mislead AI tools and directories that read it.

lucasci.com structured data, checked Nov 16, 2026
