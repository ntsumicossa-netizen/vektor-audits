Findings
01

The site never says when the store is open.

The homepage says "Come visit us at our store in Houston" and gives the address, but no page we checked lists hours. A shopper deciding whether to drive over tonight, or an AI tool asked "what gift shops in Montrose are open now," has nothing to go on from your own site.

spacemontrose.com and /pages/our-story, checked Nov 16, 2026
02

"Give us a call" appears with no phone number anywhere on the site.

The contact block reads "Give us a call or email us at hello@spacemontrose.com," but there is no number on the page and no tap to call link. Houstonia's listing shows 832-649-5743, so the number exists, it just is not on your own site.

spacemontrose.com header, checked Nov 16, 2026; houstoniamag.com/stores/space-montrose
03

A Shopify default message is still sitting in the page.

The popup section reads "Use this popup to embed a mailing list sign up form," which is the theme's placeholder text, not a real sign up offer. Search tools read that text as part of your site, and it signals a page that was never finished.

spacemontrose.com, checked Nov 16, 2026
04

Your own website did not come up when we searched your name.

A search for "Space Montrose" returned a Community Impact article, a Wanderlog page and a MapQuest listing first, plus several office rentals in Montrose, Colorado. spacemontrose.com was not in the results we saw, so the page you control is not the one people land on.

Web search for "Space Montrose", Nov 16, 2026
05

"About Us" opens the corporate gifting page instead of your story.

The About Us menu link goes to a page at /pages/squaring-the-circle-logo titled "Corporate Gifting And Curated Gift Boxes." Your actual story from Leila and Carlos sits one level down under "Our Story," so most visitors and search tools never reach it.

spacemontrose.com navigation, checked Nov 16, 2026
06

Corporate gift box searches go to national kitting companies.

You offer custom corporate gifts and curated boxes, but a search for local Houston corporate gift boxes showed Social Imprints, Cacao and Cardamom and The Artisan Gift Boxes. Your gifting page is not yet showing up for the buyers who would want it most.

Web search for "corporate gift boxes local Houston makers", Nov 16, 2026
