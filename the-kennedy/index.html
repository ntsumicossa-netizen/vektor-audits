Findings
01

Four sources, four schedules.

Your website says Tue–Wed 4pm–12am, Thu–Fri 2pm–2am, Sat 10am–2am, Sun 10am–6pm. OpenTable lists Tue–Thu opening at 2pm. AAA's listing shows weekday lunch from 11am and dinner ending at 9:45pm. Visit Houston still carries your opening-month hours. Google's AI got Sunday right in our test. Every other tool has to pick between four answers.

Source · thekennedyhouston.com, OpenTable, AAA Trip Canvas, VisitHoustonTexas.com
02

Google's AI gets your story wrong.

Asked about piano bars, Google's AI ranks you third, then describes The Kennedy as "designed like a classic Kentucky club." Asked about supper clubs with live music, it lists you fourth and calls the music "background musical curation." Your live piano is the point of the room, and the AI is underselling it.

Source · Google AI Mode, October 20, 2026
03

Your best story isn't on your homepage.

Owner Andres Castro and Ninety3 Hospitality, the East Coast supper-club concept, the piano-centered design, the Prime Meat Markets partnership: all of it lives in press articles, not on your site. AI can only repeat what it can read.

Source · thekennedyhouston.com homepage; Community Impact, Houston CityBook, OutSmart
04

Your site looks inactive to a machine.

The most recent posts in the homepage feed are holiday and New Year's Eve 2025 promotions. AI tools weigh freshness. Ten-month-old content signals a venue that may have changed or closed.

Source · thekennedyhouston.com homepage
05

The review themes AI will repeat.

OpenTable's own summaries praise the ambiance, cocktails and live music. Reviews also flag a cold room, a limited brunch menu and slow service. AI repeats both sides, and none of the complaints have a reply.

Source · OpenTable listing and review summaries
Already working
  • Hours, address and phone are published in text on your own site, not buried in an image.
  • Signature dishes (Wagyu smash burger, Wagyu tartare, caviar service) are described in plain text on the homepage.
  • Your dress code and 21+ policy are detailed enough that AI guides already quote them.
  • OpenTable shows steady booking demand and 250+ reviews.
